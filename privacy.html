Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026. This policy covers the Bit Hoops app for iPhone ("the app"), made by Jim Hollers.

The short version

Bit Hoops has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

What stays on your iPhone

Your team, settings, win-loss record, coins, Locker Room gear and purchases list are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, wins and winning margins from solo games and your achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles this data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Game Center contacts or account details.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To verify purchases and let you restore them, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history (which items you bought and when) together with a random, anonymous app ID. This information is used only to unlock what you bought and to understand purchase totals. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Game updates

The app checks for and downloads game updates (new teams, fixes) from our update site, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may briefly process your IP address to deliver the files. No personal information is sent with these requests and we do not receive or store it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: jim.hollers@icloud.com.
